Presentation Title: Instagram Ad Engagement Analysis

---

# Problem Statement (Slide 1 of 2)
Title: The Challenge of Measuring Ad Effectiveness
Content:
- In the highly competitive landscape of social media marketing, understanding how users interact with sponsored content is critical.
- Raw engagement metrics (likes, shares, simple comment counts) often fail to capture the true user sentiment and qualitative impact of an ad campaign.
- Brands struggle to differentiate between viral negative backlash and genuine positive brand engagement.
- Our goal is to move beyond superficial metrics and dive deeper into the actual text of user interactions.

---

# Problem Statement (Slide 2 of 2)
Title: Project Objective
Content:
- To develop a comprehensive, automated data science pipeline that analyzes user interactions with Instagram sponsored posts.
- Objective 1: Evaluate qualitative sentiment of user comments using Natural Language Processing (NLP).
- Objective 2: Engineer a Custom Engagement Scoring Matrix to rank ad campaigns effectively based on holistic metrics.
- Objective 3: Build predictive machine learning models to identify patterns that drive high-quality engagement.

---

# Dataset Description (Slide 1 of 1)
Title: Instagram Comments Dataset
Content:
- Source: Kaggle Dataset (`comments_cleaned.csv`)
- Description: The dataset contains cleaned, real-world user comments and interaction metadata from various Instagram ad campaigns.
- Features include: id, comment, User id, Photo id, created Timestamp, posted date, emoji used, and Hashtags used count.
- Data Prep: Pre-cleaned text data serving as the central entry point for our NLP and machine learning pipelines. 

---

# Model Used (Slide 1 of 2)
Title: Natural Language Processing (Sentiment Analysis)
Content:
- Primary Tool: TextBlob
- Purpose: To process and extract emotional context from raw comment text.
- Mechanism: Calculates comment polarity (ranging from -1.0 for highly negative to +1.0 for highly positive) and subjectivity.
- Why TextBlob?: Provides an efficient, robust lexicon-based approach to instantly gauge audience reception to specific ad campaigns without needing complex manual labeling.

---

# Model Used (Slide 2 of 2)
Title: Predictive Machine Learning Shootout
Content:
- Algorithms Used: XGBoost, LightGBM, and Scikit-Learn baseline models.
- Purpose: To predict high-engagement ad campaigns based on aggregated features and sentiment scores.
- Why these models?: 
  - XGBoost offers robust handling of complex, non-linear feature interactions and high performance.
  - LightGBM provides highly efficient, fast training on large datasets with categorical features.
- These tree-based ensemble methods are ideal for tabular engagement data and feature importance analysis.

# Efforts & Methodology (Slide 2 of 4)
Title: Phase 1 - Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
Content:
- Execution: `01_Exploratory_Data_Analysis.ipynb`
- Focus: Investigated data distributions, missing values, and high-level trends across different ad campaigns.
- Visualizations: Utilized Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn to generate correlation matrices, engagement histograms, and time-series interaction plots.
- Outcome: Established a firm understanding of baseline metrics before applying complex models.

# Efforts & Methodology (Slide 3 of 4)
Title: Phase 2 - Sentiment Analysis Implementation
Content:
- Execution: `02_Sentiment_Analysis.ipynb`
- Methodology: Passed all cleaned comments through the TextBlob NLP engine.
- Feature Creation: Generated new columns for 'Sentiment_Polarity' and 'Sentiment_Subjectivity'.
- Grouping: Aggregated sentiment scores on a per-ad basis to calculate the "Average Campaign Sentiment".

---

# Efforts & Methodology (Slide 4 of 4)
Title: Phase 3 - Feature Engineering & Scoring
Content:
- Execution: `03_Feature_Engineering_and_Scoring.ipynb`
- Custom Engagement Scoring Matrix: Combined quantitative metrics (likes, comment volume) with qualitative metrics (average sentiment polarity).
- Created a weighted formula to generate a single "Engagement Score" for ranking.
- Prepared the final feature set for the predictive ML shootout (XGBoost/LightGBM) to evaluate feature importance.

---

# Results & Analysis (Slide 1 of 4)
Title: Sentiment vs. Raw Engagement
Content:
- Finding: High comment volume does not strictly correlate with high campaign success.
- Several ads with the highest interaction rates had predominantly negative (+0.0 to -1.0) sentiment scores (e.g., controversial or polarizing ads).
- The inclusion of NLP drastically shifted the ranking of the "Top Performing Ads" compared to using raw counts alone.

---

# Results & Analysis (Slide 2 of 4)
Title: Custom Engagement Matrix Outcomes
Content:
- The engineered "Engagement Score" provided a much clearer picture of brand health.
- Top ranked campaigns successfully balanced high interaction volume with positive user sentiment (> +0.4 polarity).
- Allowed us to distinctly separate "Viral Positive" campaigns from "Viral Negative" campaigns.

---

# Results & Analysis (Slide 3 of 4)
Title: Machine Learning Model Performance
Content:
- "Predictive ML Shootout" Results:
- Both XGBoost and LightGBM models successfully learned the patterns of high-scoring ads.
- Evaluation Metrics: Models achieved strong accuracy/R2 scores depending on the target variable threshold.
- LightGBM showed slightly faster training times, while XGBoost offered marginal gains in handling outliers.

---

# Results & Analysis (Slide 4 of 4)
Title: Feature Importance Insights
Content:
- Extracted feature importance from the tree-based models.
- Key Insight: 'Sentiment_Polarity' and specific interaction ratios (e.g., reply-to-comment ratio) were among the strongest predictors of a top-tier ad campaign.
- Recommendation: Marketing teams should optimize for content that drives positive conversational threads rather than simple "like" farming.
